Meet the DJ

"...my roots run deep into Oak Cliff and Tamaulipas."

I was born in Dallas, but my roots run deep into Oak Cliff and Tamaulipas, Mexico.

The foundation of everything I do behind the booth are the sounds I grew up hearing — from the kitchen radio, to the porch where the neighbor's music carried through the walls, to every family party in Mexico.

I blend 214 Hip-Hop with the Latin hits that make your tías scream and your tíos cry. I create experiences for couples, families, and brands — where different cultures dance at the same time.
