The Experience

What it's actually like to have me in the room.

Every generation in your family gets a moment on the floor. That takes preparation before I show up, attention while I'm there, and a setup that holds together the whole night. Here's how I get there.

01

I Curate Your Sound, Not Just Mine

You're booking me because you like my style — but I also want your personal taste to come through. I'm working the night before I ever step foot in your venue, learning what you love and building a set that feels like yours. This isn't a playlist I bring to every event. It's yours.

02

I Read the Room — Every Time

Ten years of performing, behind the booth and on a stage, taught me when to stay on a vibe and when to switch it up. I'm not just playing songs. I'm watching how you and your guests move, and adjusting in real time so the energy hits right from start to finish.

03

I Think Through Every Detail

The music is the heart, but the setup matters too. Lights, sound, where I'm positioned in the room — I think through all of it so the whole experience feels intentional from the moment your guests walk in. You get a DJ who cares about the full picture, not just the next track.

Questions I Get a Lot

How far will you travel?

Dallas / Fort Worth is home base, but I travel across Texas for the right event. Tell me where and I'll tell you what it takes.

What's the deposit and booking window?

A deposit holds your date, due at booking. I take a limited number of events per weekend, so earlier is better — especially for wedding season.

Can I give you a do-not-play list?

Always. Send me your must-plays and your do-not-plays and I'll build around both.

Do you MC in Spanish and English?

Yes — announcements and transitions in both, so nobody in the room is left out.

What happens if your equipment fails?

I bring backup gear to every event. The night doesn't stop for a cable.

How far in advance should I book?

Six to twelve months out for weddings, a few months out for most other events. If your date is close, ask anyway.

Every event is quoted individually — hours, location, and what your night needs. No flat price list.
